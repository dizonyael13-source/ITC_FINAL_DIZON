Campaign Playbook

Campus Safety Campaign

Practical response steps covering natural disasters, active threats/school shootings, school bullying, school harassment, and anti-hazing / fraternity-related safety.

1. Disasters (Earthquakes, Fires, Typhoons & Flooding)

Earthquakes

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On away from glass, heavy shelves, and overhead fixtures.
  • Do not use elevators; exit calmly once shaking stops.
  • Move to an open assembly area clear of falling debris or power lines.
Click for more details & campus links →

Fires & Smoke

  • Sound the alarm and use the nearest clear stairway—never elevators.
  • Stay low under smoke and check closed doors with the back of your hand before opening.
  • Report to your designated assembly point so headcounts stay accurate.
Click for more details & campus links →

Typhoons & Manila Floods

  • Avoid wading through floodwaters or touching flooded electrical posts/outlets.
  • Monitor official PAGASA and NDRRMC advisories before commuting.
  • Stay on campus or move to a safe elevated area until conditions clear.

2. School Shooting / Active Threat Response

Run (Evacuate)

  • If a safe exit path exists, leave belongings behind and evacuate immediately.
  • Keep hands visible and follow instructions from security or police responders.
Click for more details & campus links →

Hide (Shelter & Lock Down)

  • If evacuation is unsafe, move out of view behind solid walls or heavy furniture.
  • Lock and barricade doors, silence phones (turn off vibration), and stay quiet.
Click for more details & campus links →

Fight & Call for Help

  • Only as a last resort when life is in immediate danger, act decisively as a group using available objects.
  • Call 911 or local police once safe and share exact building/floor details.
Click for more details & campus links →

3. School Bullying

Recognize & Document

  • Repeated verbal insults, exclusion, intimidation, or online/group-chat targeting all count as bullying.
  • Save screenshots, dates, and witness details safely without escalating conflict.
Click for more details & campus links →

Bystander Action

  • Support the targeted student, avoid sharing humiliating posts, and alert a trusted authority.
  • Report patterns early so student affairs or guidance can intervene.
Click for more details & campus links →

4. School Harassment

Unwanted Conduct & Boundaries

  • Any unwelcome sexual remarks, staring, touching, stalking, or online harassment violates campus safety.
  • Prioritize your immediate physical and emotional safety and move to a populated or guarded area.
Click for more details & campus links →

Reporting & Support

  • Seek help from campus security, guidance/counseling, or student welfare offices.
  • For severe or criminal incidents, coordinate with Manila police or women/children protection desks.
Click for more details & campus links →

5. Anti-Hazing & Fraternity-Related Safety

Zero Tolerance & Warning Signs

  • Physical paddling, forced alcohol/substance intake, sleep deprivation, and humiliating initiation rites are illegal and strictly prohibited.
  • Avoid secret off-campus recruitment meetups, unaccredited groups, or pressure to hide injuries.
Click for more details & campus links →

Safe Refusal & Reporting

  • Walk away immediately from any initiation threat, coercion, or fraternity-related altercation and stay in guarded campus areas.
  • Report recruitment coercion, threats, or injuries right away to OSA, Guidance, Campus Security, or 911.
