Evacuation Exits & Assembly Guidance

Perimeter & Street Exits

  • San Marcelino Street Exits: Serve the northern SV side and central campus access.
  • Romualdez Street Exits: Serve the southern ST / OZ / CT side.
  • Zobel Street & Bridge Crossings: Serve the eastern CS side and cross-campus links.
  • Note: Confirm posted fire exits and open assembly zones with campus marshals during drills or emergencies.

Evacuation Rules

  • Exit via the nearest unobstructed stairwell or gate—never use elevators during fires or quakes.
  • Avoid bottlenecked bridges or flooded low-lying streets if unsafe.
  • Stay clear of emergency vehicle lanes once outside.
